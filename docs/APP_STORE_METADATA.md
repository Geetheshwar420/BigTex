### Description:
BigTex makes it effortless to create bold, eye-catching text displays and banner signs right from your phone!

Whether you need to grab someone's attention across a noisy room, create a digital signboard at an event, display a quick message, or design striking visual banners, BigTex delivers big, beautiful typography instantly.

KEY FEATURES:
• Smooth Pinch-to-Zoom & Drag Placement: Effortlessly scale, position, and arrange text anywhere on the screen.
• Rich Color Palettes & Dark Mode: Switch between clean light backgrounds, sleek dark mode, or vibrant custom color palettes.
• Google Fonts Integration: Choose from top-tier modern typography.
• High-Res Export & Sharing: Capture and export crisp, high-resolution graphics to your photo gallery or share directly to social media.
• 100% Offline & Private: No sign-ups or internet required. Everything processes locally on your device.

Download BigTex today and let your text make a big statement!
